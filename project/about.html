About Us

ImageResizer is a free online tool that resizes images right in your browser. The idea is simple: get your images to the exact size you need in seconds, with no sign-up and no privacy trade-offs.

Our Mission

Whether you are sizing a photo for a website, a social post, an email attachment, or an online form, getting the dimensions right should take seconds. So the tool opens instantly, asks for no account, and leaves your files entirely in your own hands.

How It Works

Unlike most online image tools, this one does the work inside your browser. Your images are never uploaded to a server — the resize happens on your own device, which is both faster and more private.

What We Offer

  • Support for JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF image formats
  • Custom width and height with an optional aspect-ratio lock
  • Real-time preview so you can see the result before downloading
  • A JPG quality slider to balance clarity against file size
  • A clean, ad-supported interface — free for everyone

Our Commitment to Privacy

Because all processing happens in your browser, we never store, collect, or even see your images. The Privacy Policy has the full details.
